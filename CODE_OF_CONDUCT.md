# Code of Conduct

QGIS FMV follows the **[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.

**Report concerns:** franka1986@gmail.com

Related: [QGIS Community Conduct](https://qgis.org/community/conduct/)

---

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, education, status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or orientation.

---

## Expected behavior

- Welcoming, inclusive language
- Respect for differing viewpoints
- Graceful acceptance of constructive criticism
- Focus on what benefits the community
- Empathy toward others

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome advances
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks
- Harassment in public or private
- Publishing private information without consent
- Other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate these standards and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

Report incidents to **franka1986@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially.
